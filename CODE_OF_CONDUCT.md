# Code of conduct

Headroom is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to contribute, whatever your experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and constructive.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/medaimane), or report the content to GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who do so repeatedly or seriously.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
